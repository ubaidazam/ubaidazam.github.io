About

Trustworthy AI for high-stakes decisions

I build machine learning systems that can tell when their output should be trusted. My work combines Bayesian uncertainty for language models, explanations that are tested for faithfulness rather than assumed, and privacy-preserving adaptation for settings where data cannot be pooled. I have applied it to clinical and legal text, online harms and under-represented languages.

I am the primary researcher on PICASO, a competitively awarded Alan Turing Institute grant, and I led the TrustWeave team in the United Nations Development Programme's AI Trust and Safety Re-Imagination Programme. I am open to research scientist and applied scientist roles in academia and industry.

Research questions

How confident should this prediction be?

Bayesian kernel methods and deep Gaussian processes that give language models calibrated confidence, so low-confidence cases go to a person instead of through as settled.

WSDM 2025 COLING 2025 WISE 2024

Does the explanation match what the model did?

Span-level rationales for harmful content, evaluated for plausibility and faithfulness, and stress-tested under adversarial variation and shift over time.

IJCNN 2026 LREC 2022

Can we adapt LLMs when data can't move?

Lightweight federated prompt tuning that adapts language models across clients and languages without centralising sensitive data.

LREC 2026 Findings of EMNLP 2023

How sure is a fact in a knowledge graph?

Uncertainty-aware knowledge graph embeddings on conceptual spaces, where the geometry carries both meaning and confidence, plus an open Wikidata-derived benchmark.

CIKM 2026 Code & data

Featured · CIKM 2026, Rome

PROCS: Uncertainty-Aware Knowledge Graph Embeddings via Conceptual Spaces

Knowledge graph embeddings that represent each entity as a region in a conceptual space, so a predicted fact comes with a calibrated measure of how far it can be relied on. Released with Defence-Wikidata, an open benchmark built from a full Wikidata dump.

U. Azam, I. Razzak, S. Schockaert, S. Jameel · to appear at CIKM 2026, presenting in Rome in November
